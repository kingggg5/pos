# Smart POS Design System — Professional Clean Light Mode

<!-- impeccable:design-system 3 -->

## Product Direction

The interface is a **professional, high-contrast POS command center** with a crisp, comfortable light background (`#f8fafc` / `#f1f5f9`), solid white elevated card surfaces, precise micro-borders, and inline vector SVG iconography. It looks clean, clinical, enterprise-grade, and ultra-readable for retail cashiers.

## 60 / 30 / 10 Color System (Clean Light Palette)

- **60% foundation**: Crisp light slate background (`#f8fafc`), clean surface cards (`#ffffff`), subtle borders (`#e2e8f0` / `#cbd5e1`).
- **30% operational structure**: Deep navy headings & body text (`#0f172a` / `#334155`), slate secondary text (`#64748b`), indigo-blue data accents (`#4f46e5` / `#6366f1`).
- **10% action & primary focus**: Vivid brand accent (`#2563eb` Royal Blue or `#f97316` Amber Orange for checkout actions), giving high contrast and high clarity.

## Typography & Iconography

- Font stack: `Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Noto Sans Thai`, sans-serif.
- Headings: 18px–24px, weight 700–800.
- Body & inputs: 13px–14px, weight 500–600.
- Iconography: **100% Inline SVG Vector Icons**. Zero emojis across the entire platform.

## Shape & Surface

- Card rounding: `--radius-sm: 8px`, `--radius-md: 12px`, `--radius-lg: 16px`.
- Solid white cards with soft drop shadows (`0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`).
- Crisp focus outlines (`2px solid #2563eb`).

## Touch & Cashier Ergonomics

- Touch targets: Minimum **44px height** for all controls.
- Checkout button: **52px height** — primary visual anchor.
